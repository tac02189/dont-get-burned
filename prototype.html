The spine of this talk

Four questions — asked before you trust the scan, not after it burned you.

01
Did I obtain an adequate examination?
Bowel gas, habitus, incomplete sweeps. An inadequate study is not a negative study.
02
What am I actually seeing?
Artifact and anatomic mimic, together — because at the bedside you cannot tell them apart by category.
03
What does this finding establish?
A finding supports a conclusion. It rarely establishes one on its own.
04
What remains unanswered?
The question you never asked is the one that harms the patient.
Why not "the four liars"

"Which one fooled me?" is a question you ask afterwards. These four you can ask with the probe still on the patient.
